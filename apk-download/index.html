Install note

  • On Android, your device may ask to allow apps from this source or the Files app.
  • Review the permissions and continue only if the request matches the PSM Office app.
  • After installation, open the app and sign in with your valid PSM Office account.

Employee sign-in

After your account is created, check the email address you gave the office—including spam or junk—for an account email containing your Employee ID and temporary password. Sign in with those details, then change your password after your first login. If no email arrives, verify your address with the office or admin and ask them to check whether the credentials email was sent successfully.
